Research Introduction

ScienceBridge AI is an educational artificial intelligence research initiative focused on analyzing argumentative reasoning quality using transformer-based natural language processing systems.

The project investigates whether machine learning models can evaluate analytical depth, logical coherence, evidence integration, and communication clarity in student-written arguments.

By combining educational research with modern AI systems, ScienceBridge AI aims to develop tools that support critical thinking, reasoning development, and computational literacy in future learning environments.

Research Objective

Traditional educational evaluation systems primarily measure factual recall and correctness. However, higher-order cognitive abilities such as analytical reasoning, evidence integration, and argumentative coherence are substantially more difficult to evaluate at scale.

ScienceBridge AI investigates whether transformer-based NLP architectures can provide meaningful assessment signals related to reasoning quality and argumentative sophistication in student writing.
